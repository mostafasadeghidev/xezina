Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Xezina is a browser extension. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your bookmarks or any other data.

What Xezina reads

Where your data goes

Google user data

When you connect Google Drive, Xezina requests only the drive.file scope and keeps its data in a “Xezina” folder in your Drive. It can only see files it created itself; it cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Data from Google APIs is used only to store and read your own Xezina sync files, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Xezina’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

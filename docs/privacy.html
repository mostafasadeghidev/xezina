Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Xezina is a browser extension. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your bookmarks or any other data.

What Xezina reads

  • Your bookmarks, to show, search and sync them.
  • Pages you bookmarked — only if you allow "access to pages" — to read their title, description and preview image, to check whether links still work, and to take a screenshot of a bookmarked page while you are viewing it. Pages you have not bookmarked are never read or captured.

Where your data goes

Everything is stored in your browser. If you turn on sync, Xezina uploads your bookmarks, notes, tags and preview images to the storage you choose (your Google Drive app folder, Dropbox app folder, OneDrive app folder, your WebDAV server, your S3 bucket or your GitHub repository), using your own credentials, directly from your browser. That provider's privacy policy applies to what you store there.

With end-to-end encryption on, the data is encrypted in your browser with your passphrase (AES-256-GCM) before upload, so the storage provider cannot read it.

Google user data

When you connect Google Drive, Xezina requests only the drive.appdata scope: access to a hidden folder that only Xezina can see. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Data from Google APIs is used only to store and read your own Xezina sync files, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Xezina's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Favicons

In Chromium browsers, site icons come from the browser itself. In Firefox, if previews are enabled, icons are loaded from DuckDuckGo's public icon service using only the site's domain.

Deleting your data

Disconnect the storage in Settings and delete the Xezina folder (or bucket prefix / repository) there. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.
